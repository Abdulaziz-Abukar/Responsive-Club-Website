Pet Care Tips

1. Brush before the Zoomies

Regular brushing keeps your pet’s coat clean, reduces shedding, and gives you a chance to check for fleas or skin irritation.

2. Treats Are Not Meals

It’s tempting to spoil them, but treats should only make up 10% or less of your pet’s daily food intake. Think of them as rewards — not snacks!

3. Bath Time Basics

Most pets don’t need frequent baths. Once every few weeks (or months for cats) is enough unless they get into something messy.

4. Trim Those Claws

Long nails can cause discomfort or even injury. Clip them regularly or have a vet/groomer do it. Bonus: no more floor tap-tap sounds!

5. Safe & Cozy Spaces

Give your pet a calm space to rest and feel safe — a crate, a soft bed, or a cozy corner they can call their own.

6. Walks Are More Than Exercise

Walks help pets burn energy, but they’re also mentally stimulating. New smells = new adventures!
